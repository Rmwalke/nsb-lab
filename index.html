01 Choose an experiment

The example disturbance is h = (a sin(ky), 0, 0). It is smooth, mean-zero and divergence-free, and its A₋₁ norm is |a|/k.

Changing k changes the disturbance's spatial frequency. This is an input family, not a simulated fluid trajectory.

What this result establishes

Only the displayed finite inequalities, assuming the supplied analytic bounds are valid. A failed certificate does not mean the fluid blows up; a passing certificate is not a complete proof of the PDE theorem.

This browser checks 34 selected inequalities from a derived snapshot. The linked Python audit also checks three original input files, their dependency hashes and archived recurrence conditions. Those additional checks are not performed here; browser PASS is not the full PASS_DATA_ONLY result.

WHO CAN USE THIS?

A small tool for a precise research question.

For researchers and students who want to inspect a certificate, see how a perturbation changes its inequalities, or reproduce a reported calculation. It does not predict engineering flow, optimize equipment, or establish an industrial benefit.

TRACEABLE, WITH LIMITS

From archived inputs to a fresh calculation.

The browser recomputes the two sets of 17 inequalities with exact rational arithmetic. The original analytic enclosures, the full PDE proof, novelty and the paper's other claims remain separate questions. The app was prepared with AI assistance.
