THE MAIN CLAIM · THEOREM 2.1

An explicit family, with an explicit convergence rate.

The manuscript concerns the unforced Navier–Stokes equation at viscosity one on the normalized 2π-periodic three-dimensional torus. It takes u₀ = W + h, where W = (sin z, sin x, sin y) and h is smooth, real, mean-zero and divergence-free.

Within the stated radius, Theorem 2.1 gives a computer-assisted certificate that the ordinary Picard iteration, started from the complete heat flow etΔ(W+h), enters a common invariant set at its third iterate and converges on the whole time half-line, without restarting the iteration.

हा हस्तलिखितातील प्रमेयाचा दावा आहे. त्याची वैधता संपूर्ण गणिती युक्तिवाद आणि प्रमाणपत्रांवर अवलंबून आहे. येथील तुलना त्या संपूर्ण पुराव्याची स्वतंत्र पुनर्पडताळणी नाही.

STATED HYPOTHESIS & BOUND
A−1(h) ≤ 10−4
‖vn − u‖X ≤ 6(9/10)n−3

n ≥ 3  ·  X = L²((0,∞); A₀)

As(f) = Σk≠0 |k|s |f̂(k)|, using the Euclidean norm of each Fourier coefficient.

Smooth h may have infinitely many Fourier modes. The statement retains its smoothness assumption.

Where the contribution sits.

SEPARATE RESULT · THEOREM 3.1

A larger regularity class

The manuscript includes u₀ = aW+h with |a| ≤ 8/7 and A−1(h) ≤ 1/100, keeping the same structural and smoothness assumptions.

Swapping x and y maps Silva's cyclic field U to W. Since A−1(h) ≤ A₂(h), his class 0 ≤ a ≤ 7/10, A₂(h) ≤ 1/100 is included. The inclusion is strict: W is in the larger class, while its minimum A₂ distance from the earlier amplitude segment is 9/10.

This is a global-regularity comparison for one specified cyclic class. The main Picard convergence conclusion is not extended to this larger region.
