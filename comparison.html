WHAT THE RESULT MEANS

Useful evidence, with a precise scope.

The comparison certifies rational linear reconstructions of the saved samples. It does not directly certify each formula’s continuous exponential path. The paper’s global X-norm convergence theorem is a separate claim.

A useful result on this test does not by itself establish novelty, an industrial advantage, or a general Navier–Stokes regularity result.

READ THE COSTS CORRECTLY

Three costs, recorded separately.

Sampling an explicit formula and advancing an ODE solver are different operations. Setup and certificate generation can dominate a small benchmark. The download records the individual runs so the comparison can be inspected.
